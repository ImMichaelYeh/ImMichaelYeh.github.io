My Auto Codec Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

My Auto Codec is made by MY Development Life. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

My Auto Codec stores the following information only in the app's private on-device storage:

  • Bluetooth device addresses for devices you select.
  • Your automatic codec preferences for those devices.
  • The latest local status message shown by the app.

No transmission or sharing

My Auto Codec does not have the Internet permission. It does not transmit, upload, sell, or share your Bluetooth device addresses, codec preferences, or other information with MY Development Life or any third party.

Bluetooth permissions

The Nearby devices permission lets the app list paired Bluetooth devices and request an A2DP codec preference. Companion-device presence access lets a saved codec preference be re-applied when an approved device reconnects.

Data retention and deletion

Information remains on your device until you remove an automatic codec rule, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. The app does not maintain a server-side copy of this information.

Security

The app keeps its stored information in Android's private app storage. Because the app does not transmit this information, no Bluetooth device addresses or codec preferences are sent to a server or third party.

Your choices

You can remove an automatic codec rule at any time in the app. You can also revoke Bluetooth permission in Android Settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes the information stored by the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact MY Development Life at my.development.life@gmail.com.
