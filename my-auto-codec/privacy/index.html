My Auto Codec Privacy Policy

Revised October 14, 2026

My Auto Codec is developed by MY Development Life. The app saves data only on your phone. It does not upload, sell, or share that data. The app does not have Internet access permission.

What the app uses and saves

The app reads your Bluetooth device details, supported codecs, connection status, and media volume to display your audio settings and apply your codec choice. It saves the following information in private app storage on your phone:

  • The addresses of your selected Bluetooth devices and your automatic codec choices.
  • The latest status message, codec compatibility results, and any pending reminders to change your phone settings.
  • Your tour progress, so the app can resume the tour or stop showing the welcome prompt.
  • If rating reminders are available, a launch count of up to three, the last reminder date, and your choice to stop reminders.

The tour and FAQs work offline, without analytics or tracking.

Permissions

The “Nearby devices” permission lets the app find paired audio devices and request codec changes. With your approval, companion-device access lets the app apply your saved codec choice when a device reconnects, even when the app is closed.

Your choices and data deletion

You can remove saved codec choices, skip the tour, or replay it from “FAQs”. You can also revoke Bluetooth access in Android Settings. If a rating reminder appears, choose “Not now” to postpone it or “Don't ask again” to stop future reminders.

The app updates saved information as you use it. Removing a saved codec choice deletes that choice. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes all saved app data. We do not keep a copy of this data on a server.

Ratings, this website, and email

If the rating feature is available, tapping “Rate app” opens Google Play. Google handles any rating or review you submit under its Privacy Policy. The app does not receive or save your rating or review.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages and opens in your browser. The app's link includes its light or dark theme setting, but no saved Bluetooth data. GitHub may process information about your visit, such as your IP address, under its Privacy Statement. This page does not use analytics, tracking cookies, or browser storage.

If you email us, we use your email address and message to reply. Your email provider handles delivery under its own privacy policy. The app does not automatically attach device details or logs.
