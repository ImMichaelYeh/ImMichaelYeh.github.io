My Auto Codec Privacy Policy

Pre-release draft v0.2 · Revised October 14, 2026

My Auto Codec is made by MY Development Life. The app keeps its saved data on your phone. It does not upload, sell, or share that data, and has no Internet permission.

What the app uses and saves

The app reads Bluetooth device details, supported codecs, connection status, and media volume to show your audio setup and apply your chosen codec. It saves these items in private app storage:

  • Selected Bluetooth device addresses and automatic codec choices.
  • The latest status message, codec compatibility results, and pending phone-settings reminders.
  • Tour progress, so you can resume it or stop seeing the welcome prompt.
  • For rating reminders, where available: a launch count capped at three, the last reminder date, and whether you chose to stop reminders.

The tour and FAQs work offline, without analytics or tracking.

Permissions

“Nearby devices” access lets the app find paired audio devices and request codec changes. Companion-device access lets it restore your codec choice when an approved device reconnects, including when the app is closed.

Your choices and deleting data

You can remove a saved codec choice, skip or replay the tour from “FAQs”, and revoke Bluetooth access in Android Settings. For rating reminders, “Not now” postpones the prompt and “Don't ask again” stops it.

Saved data changes as you use the app. Removing a codec choice deletes that rule. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes all saved app data. We keep no server copy of it.

Ratings, this website, and email

“Rate app”, where available, opens Google Play. Google handles any rating or review under its Privacy Policy; the app does not receive or save it.

This policy opens in your browser on GitHub Pages. The app's link includes a light or dark theme choice, but no saved Bluetooth data. GitHub may process website requests, including your IP address, under its Privacy Statement. This page adds no analytics, tracking cookies, or browser storage.

If you email us, we receive your address and message to respond. Your email provider handles delivery under its own policy. The app attaches no device details or logs automatically.
