# Privacy policy versions

`index.html` is the latest policy and the app's permanent link. `versions/` holds
standalone HTML snapshots. Keep existing snapshots unchanged, including their
wording, dates, and theme support.

The first archived draft is v0.1. Drafts use v0.x and a revision date because the
app has not launched. At public launch, publish v1.0 with an effective date.
After launch, increment the version for each policy revision and record its
effective date.

For each revision:

1. Update `index.html`, its version/date, and its policy history with a short
   description of the change.
2. Copy it to `versions/vX.Y.html`. Add the archived-draft notice (or an archived
   policy notice after launch) linking to `/my-auto-codec/privacy/`.
3. Check both themes and the history links. Keep `data-theme-link` on internal
   policy links so they preserve the app's requested theme.
4. Commit and publish the latest page and its snapshot together.

Do not overwrite an old version or reuse a version number. These snapshots
record draft history; they do not imply a draft was publicly released or effective.
